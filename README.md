## 🧭 My Engineering Approach

I learn by turning concepts into systems:

**Problem**  
↓  
**Understand the concept**  
↓  
**Build a small experiment**  
↓  
**Integrate it into a system**  
↓  
**Evaluate it**  
↓  
**Improve**

I want to eventually be able to look at a problem and decide whether it actually needs:

`ML` · `LLMs` · `RAG` · `Tools` · `Agents` · `Workflows` · `Knowledge Graphs`

**...or none of them.**
